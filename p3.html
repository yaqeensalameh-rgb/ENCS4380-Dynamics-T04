First-order model: G(s) = 5 / (0.15s + 1). K = 500 RPM ÷ 100% is an ideal no-load approximation; τ = 0.15 s is an assumed value. Neither was obtained from an encoder/PWM step measurement. Motor speed may be lower under load.

Component source: Micromouse Components. Before the “My System” submission, replace assumed K and τ with experimentally measured values and document the test.

02 · CONTROLLER

Digital PID Parameters

Discrete PID: u[k] = Kp·e[k] + Ki·Ts·Σe[j] + Kd·(e[k] − e[k−1])/Ts. Output is saturated to 0–100% PWM. The controller updates only at sample instants.

Generated for the current gains, Ts, and setpoint. Uses a DRV8833 input in forward-PWM mode and ESP32 Arduino PWM API. Encoder pulses per wheel revolution and actual GPIO wiring must be confirmed.
