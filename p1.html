02 · CONTROLLER

PID Controller Parameters

P

Reacts to the current error. Faster response, but leaves a steady-state error.

I

Accumulates past error. Removes the steady-state error, but can add overshoot.

D

Reacts to how fast the output changes. Damps overshoot.

Control law: u = Kp·e + Ki·∫e dt − Kd·dy/dt (derivative on the measurement, so a setpoint step does not cause a spike).

08 · SYSTEM STRUCTURE

Closed-Loop Block Diagram

The output is measured and subtracted from the setpoint. The PID acts on the error, and the actuator limit clips the control signal. Integral anti-windup (conditional integration) is active while the actuator is saturated.
